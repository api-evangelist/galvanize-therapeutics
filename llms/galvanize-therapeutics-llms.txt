# Galvanize Therapeutics

> Galvanize Therapeutics, Inc. is a Redwood City, California medical device company founded in 2015 by Jonathan Waldstreicher, MD and William Krimsky, MD, and created and incubated by Apple Tree Partners (ATP). Galvanize develops pulsed electric field (PEF) therapies that deliver high-voltage, short-duration energy locally to alter the transmembrane potential of targeted cells, inducing non-thermal regulated cell death without denaturing cellular proteins or the extracellular matrix. Its Aliya PEF System is cleared for surgical ablation of soft tissue and is being studied in non-small cell lung cancer; RheOx addresses chronic bronchitis in the airway. As of this profile the company publishes no public API, SDK, developer portal, or machine-readable interface.
